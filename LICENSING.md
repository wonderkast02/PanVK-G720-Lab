# Licensing and third-party material

This repository is an evidence and research archive. It does **not** apply one
blanket license to every historical item.

- Upstream-derived Mesa/Panfrost/PanVK/Panfork code snippets, patches and
  notices retain their original upstream licenses and copyright notices.
- Logs and evidence do not transfer ownership of third-party code.
- Third-party artifacts retain their own license terms and must not be treated
  as unrestricted merely because a hash or diagnostic record exists here.
- Project-authored material that does not contain an explicit license grant
  must not be assumed to grant unrestricted redistribution or modification.
- Existing SPDX identifiers and upstream notices must be preserved when
  applicable.

No file should be redistributed solely to make the archive look complete.
When redistribution rights are uncertain, publish provenance, hashes and
reproduction instructions instead of the binary/source payload.
