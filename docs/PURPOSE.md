# Purpose and authority boundary

`wonderkast02/panvk-g720-kbase-csf` is the source authority for driver code,
branches, tags and releases.

`wonderkast02/PanVK-G720-Lab` is the evidence authority for research history,
hardware validation, diagnostics, manifests, causal investigations and
reproducibility records.

The Lab must not become a duplicate Mesa source tree or silently redefine a
driver release. Runtime adapters such as Winlator/Vortek and consumers such as
DXVK/VKD3D are evidence layers, not the PanVK core.
