# ADR-0008 — Publish the Lab as an evidence archive

Status: accepted — 2026-09-21

## Decision

The Lab transitions from private technical memory to a public research and
evidence archive, while the driver source authority remains
`wonderkast02/panvk-g720-kbase-csf`.

## Constraints

- the public beta identity is immutable;
- historical records are preserved and explicitly contextualized;
- no duplicate Mesa source tree becomes authority;
- secrets and unnecessary personal metadata are removed before publication;
- upstream copyright/license notices are preserved;
- public claims remain bounded by hardware and runtime evidence.
